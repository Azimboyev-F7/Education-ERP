# Product

IELTS teaching centre platform with two apps on one API.

- **Staff Console** (`apps/admin`): administrators, academic managers and teachers track how students are performing and run the teaching loop: groups, assignments, the Writing/Speaking review queue, content library, accounts.
- **Student App** (`apps/student`): students train for IELTS on their own and complete assigned work.

## Users and scene

Staff console: teachers and managers at a desk or between classes, on a laptop, in daylight, working through lists (who is behind, what needs marking). They return daily and want to find the next thing to do in seconds. Not designers, not patient with decoration.

## Tone

Calm, precise, quietly confident. Apple-like restraint: generous whitespace, hairline borders, one accent colour, type doing the work. Plain English, active voice, controls named for their action.

## Principles

1. The next action is always visible: what needs marking, who is overdue.
2. Scores are honest: auto-scored accuracy, teacher bands and band estimates are never merged, and estimates are labelled as estimates.
3. Scope is invisible: people only see their groups and students; empty states say so plainly.
4. Numbers are tabular, dates are formatted for the reader, nothing relies on colour alone.
